## 2. Holdout Bootstrap PRNG and Predeclared Comparisons (`SPEC_v1.md` §7)

Before running holdout rollouts, the bootstrap resampler is frozen as follows:

- **Algorithm:** `sha256-rejection-u64-v1` (`ip_research.uniform.uniform_integer`) with `bootstrap_seed = 4000`, `replicates = 10000`, and `max_words_per_draw = 64`.
- **Index generation (`b = 0, ..., 9999`):**
  - Repetition indices (`10` draws with replacement from `0..9`): key prefix `synthetic-pid-v1|bootstrap|4000|{b}|repetition|{i}` for `i = 0, ..., 9`.
  - Holdout rollout-seed indices (`30` draws with replacement from `0..29` corresponding to seeds `1000..1029`): key prefix `synthetic-pid-v1|bootstrap|4000|{b}|rollout_seed|{j}` for `j = 0, ..., 29`.
  - Identical resampled index tuples `(rep_indices[b], seed_indices[b])` are shared across all comparisons.
- **Seed-block aggregation:** For each method/repetition $q$ and holdout seed $s \in \{1000, \dots, 1029\}$, $J[q, s]$ is the equal-weight mean of total rollout cost across the three scenarios (`nominal`, `disturbance-shift`, `saturation-limited`). The three scenarios are never split across bootstrap draws.
- **Crossed paired bootstrap estimator:** For comparator pair $(A, B)$ with $D[q, s] = J_A[q, s] - J_B[q, s]$, replicate $b$ computes:
  $$\bar{D}^{*(b)} = \frac{1}{10 \times 30} \sum_{i=0}^{9} \sum_{j=0}^{29} D\big[\text{rep\_indices}[b][i],\, \text{seed\_indices}[b][j]\big]$$
  For single-repetition (`seed 2000`) paired comparisons across the 30 holdout seed blocks (`D[s] = J_A[2000, s] - J_B[2000, s]`), replicate $b$ uses the exact same `seed_indices[b]` vector:
  $$\bar{D}_{2000}^{*(b)} = \frac{1}{30} \sum_{j=0}^{29} D\big[\text{seed\_indices}[b][j]\big]$$
- **Quantile endpoints:** Sort the `10,000` bootstrap estimates in ascending order and interpolate linearly at $(n - 1) p$ (`n = 10000`) for $p \in \{0.0125, 0.9875\}$ (`97.5%` two-sided interval per comparator, Bonferroni familywise $\alpha = 0.05$ across the two primary comparators `B1` and `B2`), plus secondary descriptive `95%` endpoints $p \in \{0.025, 0.975\}$.
- **Decision rule and single-repetition live scope:**
  - Under `SPEC_v1.md` §6–§7, the 10-repetition confirmatory claim for `A-live-proposer` requires all 10 search repetitions (`2000..2009`). Because repetitions `2001..2009` were preregistered as `not_run` in `LIVE_v1.md`, the 10-repetition confirmatory gate is strictly recorded as **`inconclusive-incomplete-search-repetitions`** (`1/10` live repetitions executed).
  - For `A-mock` (`10/10` repetitions executed), the 10-repetition crossed bootstrap against `B1` and `B2` is evaluated under the §7 decision rule and tagged `llm_efficacy: "not-tested"`.
  - **Seed-2000 live holdout gate (exploratory, single repetition).** Two primary comparisons, mirroring the two SPEC comparators:
    - **P1:** `A-live-proposer (2000)` vs. `B1-uniform-random` over all 10 repetitions, `crossed` design (the single live row is repeated across the 10 repetition slots; only `B1` repetitions vary under resampling).
    - **P2:** `A-live-proposer (2000)` vs. `B2-coordinate-search`, `seed-block` design (B2 is one deterministic path).
    - `supports-seed2000-holdout-direction` iff both 97.5% upper bounds are `< 0`, `A-live` has zero holdout numerical failures, and its mean unsafe rate is no greater than each comparator's.
    - `contradicts-seed2000-holdout-direction` iff either 97.5% lower bound is `>= 0` or `A-live`'s mean unsafe rate exceeds either comparator's.
    - `inconclusive-numerical-failure` if any `A-live` holdout rollout fails numerically; otherwise `inconclusive`.
  - **Secondary, descriptive seed-2000 comparisons (`seed-block`):** `A-live` vs. `B1 (2000)`, `A-live-no-history`, `A-mock`, `B0-fixed`; `A-live-no-history` vs. `B2` and vs. `B1` (10 reps, crossed). Plus the five anti-windup holdout pairs (`no_aw − aw`). These do not change the gate.
  - **Full 10-repetition comparisons (`crossed`):** `B2` vs. `B1`, `A-mock` vs. `B1`, `A-mock` vs. `B2` (`A-mock` tagged `llm_efficacy: "not-tested"`).
  - **Known limitation, stated in advance:** the crossed P1 interval omits the live proposer's own search-to-search variance (only one live repetition exists), so it understates uncertainty for `A-live`. A favorable gate is therefore exploratory evidence about one frozen controller, not evidence about the proposer method in general.
  - **Holdout-access consequence:** after this run, seeds `1000..1029` count as inspected. Any later tuning must be labeled exploratory, and confirmation would need a new untouched split under a new benchmark version.

## 3. Holdout Access Log & Saved Example Trajectories (`Phase 4.2`)

- **Explicit opt-in & access log:** Holdout execution requires `--enable-holdout` on `experiments/run_holdout.py`. A `holdout-access-started` entry is appended to `reports/holdout-comparison-v1/holdout_access_log.jsonl` *before* the first holdout draw, and a `holdout-access-completed` entry (holdout noise draws, rollouts, network attempts) after. The runner refuses to overwrite an existing holdout `summary.json`; the evaluation is one-shot. Network calls are denied during the run. Standard unit and smoke tests use fixtures only (`0` holdout draws).
- **Aggregation:** holdout means use the same arithmetic as the pinned development aggregator (equal mean over seeds within a scenario, then equal mean across scenarios), applied to the ordered holdout grid. Identical candidates (deterministic B2/A-mock repeats) are simulated once and reused; they are not counted as independent samples.
- **Saved example trajectories (`trajectories.json` / `trajectories.csv`):** for `PLAN.md` task 4.2, without post hoc reconstruction, the runner saves complete per-step trajectories (`t = 0..199`: state, next state, reference, error, action, raw action, integral, integration-blocked flag, disturbance, noise, saturation flag) on `development seed 0` and `holdout seed 1000` across all three scenarios for `B0-fixed`, `B1-uniform-random (seed 2000)`, `B2-coordinate-search`, `A-mock`, `A-live-no-history (seed 2000)`, and `A-live-proposer (seed 2000)`.
