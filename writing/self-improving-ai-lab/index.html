The Self-Improving AI Lab

Agentic research, autonomous discovery, and iterative optimization, examined through hypotheses, experiments, and evidence.

THE GUIDING QUESTION

What would it take for an AI system to improve its own research process?

I explore how research loops generate hypotheses, choose experiments, and learn from failures. Recursive self-improvement is a question to define and test; bounded optimization alone does not establish it.
