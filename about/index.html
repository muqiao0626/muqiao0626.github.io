ABOUT

Mu Qiao, PhD

AI Researcher & Engineer.

From neuroscience to autonomous research

My path began in neuroscience, studying retinal circuits and the relationships between neuronal cell types, gene expression, and connectivity. I earned my PhD at Harvard and continued in computational neuroscience as a Swartz-Sloan Postdoctoral Fellow at Caltech.

Working with biological data drew me toward interpretable models of learning and representation. That thread continues in my research on neuronal connectivity, cell-type matching, and the relationship between classical learning rules and linear attention.

Engineering brought a different set of questions: how do learning systems behave under uncertainty, delayed feedback, and practical constraints? My work at Apple, LinkedIn, and Meta has spanned adaptive interaction, optimization, LLM post-training, and evaluation. I am currently a staff machine learning engineer at Apple.

I am now exploring autonomous AI research: how systems might propose hypotheses, design useful tests, and improve from experimental feedback. I want to understand both what makes these loops promising and where their claims need stronger evidence.
