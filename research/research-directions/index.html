Research directions

Open questions connecting learning theory, autonomous research, and practical optimization.

2026-10-16 · Updated 2026-10-26

Learning and representation

How do learning rules shape what a system can represent? My work on linear attention studies explicit mathematical connections to classical conditioning models. The preprint distinguishes algebraic equivalences from empirical observations; the broader relationship between biological and artificial learning remains an open research direction.

My work on bilinear models of neuronal connectivity and optimal-transport cell-type matching asks a related question: how can a simple, interpretable model reveal structure in biological data?

Hypotheses that can fail

Can a system use a history of experiments to propose a better next test? A useful demonstration needs a restricted task, explicit comparisons, and a way for the proposed method to lose. Improvement should be judged against a shared evaluation budget and evidence that was not used to select the solution.

Optimization with constraints

Performance is only one part of a useful system. Bounds, stability, reproducibility, and failure cases belong in the evaluation. The completed exploratory demonstration uses an independent synthetic control problem with no connection asserted to any employer system.

See the synthetic controller-search project and its results and diagnostic figures. The comparison evaluates bounded search methods at matched candidate budgets. A separate five-search replication found lower mean holdout cost than the registered random and coordinate baselines on fresh noise cases within the same task family. This does not establish transfer to new tasks or improvement of the research procedure.

Disclosure

This page describes research interests. The linked independent synthetic study reports exploratory results and their limitations; it does not establish general proposer superiority or recursive self-improvement.
